Separation of internal and interaction dynamics for NLS-described wave packets with different carrier waves

ABSTRACT:

We give a detailed analysis of the interaction of two NLS-described wave packets with different carrier waves for a nonlinear wave equation. By separating the internal dynamics of each wave packet from the dynamics caused by the interaction we prove that there is almost no interaction of such wave packets. We also prove the validity of a formula for the envelope shift caused by the interaction of the wave packets. We explain the corresponding result in periodic media and in FPU lattices. This joint work with Martina Chirilus-Bruckner, Christopher Chong, and Hannes Uecker.
