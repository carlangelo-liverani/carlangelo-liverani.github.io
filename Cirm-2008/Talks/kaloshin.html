Vadim Kaloshin

Various examples of Arnold diffusion

ABSTRACT:

1. A simple example of Arnold diffusion and coupled pendulums.
2. Further examples of Arnold diffusion: nearest neighbor coupled pendulums, large/infinite dimensional systems in a normal form (FPU, NLS).
3. Another mechanics of diffusion for large dimensional systems.
4. Geometry of a priori unstable systems.
5. Examples of a priori unstable systems in celestial mechanics.
