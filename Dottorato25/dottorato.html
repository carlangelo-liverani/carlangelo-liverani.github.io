COURSE PROGRAM

I will try to provide some notes for the material that cannot be easily found in print. However, most of the topics can be found in:

  • To know more about Lyapunov Exponents: Lectures on Lyapunov Exponents. Marcelo Viana, Cambridge University Press, (2014)
  • To know more about billiards: Chaotic Billiards. Nikolai Chernov and Roberto Markarian, AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs Volume: 127; 316 pp (2006)
  • On how to establish ergodicity for Hamiltonian systems: Ergodicity in Hamiltonian Systems, C.Liverani, M. Wojtkowski, Dynamics Reported, 4, pp. 130-202, (1995)
  • To know more about foliation regularity read Appendix B of this article .
  • For a quick introduction to Transfer Operators: Transfer operators in Hyperbolic Dynamics An introduction, C.Liverani, M. Demers and N. Kiamari. 33 Colloquio Brasilero de Matematica. Editora do IMPA. pp.252 (2021)
