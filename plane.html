Arriving by plane: from the Airport to Rome

If arriving by plane, you will land at Roma Fiumicino - Leonardo da Vinci airport , which is located near the tirrenic coast, south-west of Rome. The best way to reach dowtown Rome is by train :  the train station at Fiumicino Airport is directly linked to the terminals, and easy to reach (you may check the Airport map ) . We suggest to take the direct train to:
 Stazione Termini   ( about 30 minutes from Fiumicino Airport)

At night there is a bus service that brings you to Stazione Tiburtina .  Taxis   from the Airport are very expensive and subject to the heavy roman traffic.

Once in Rome

You can take a taxi to your destination, or use public transportation


Tickets have to be purchased in advance, and always validated :
       before boarding the train, using the yellow machines at the stations
       when entering the subway station
       right after boarding the bus
