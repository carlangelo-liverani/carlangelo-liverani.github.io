REGULAR AND UNSTABLE MOTION IN HAMILTONIAN SYSTESM
Roma, 5-9 September 2000
      The Conference
The Conference aims at presenting the various aspects of Hamiltonian Dynamics with particular emphasis on future perspectives.
The conference will take place at the University of Rome III.
