If you get to the airport then take a bus for Trieste (they depart after each plane arrival). The bus stops close to the train station.

To go to SISSA from Trieste on can take the Bus 36 from the train station. The bus follows the coastline, you have to get out just after the bus goes under two tunnels. After you stop you backtract to get to the Guest House Galileo (behind the ITCP--main building in the picture).

At the guest house Galileo there is a reception open 24 hours a day that will assign you to your rooms.

Upon arrival, on Monday, please go the the SISSA secretary (first floor) to fill the needed forms and to obtain the vouchers for the meals.
