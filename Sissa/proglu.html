Luzzatto: Nonuniformly Hyperbolic Dynamics

This course will introduce the notion of a "non-uniformly hyperbolic" dynamical system, which can be very loosely described as a system with non-zero Lyapunov exponents. This includes "uniformly hyperbolic" systems as special cases, and the techniques for studying such systems are very much based on techniques developed for uniformly hyperbolic systems, for which much stronger results have been and can be obtained.
A powerful abstract theory of non-uniformly hyperbolic systems has been developed by Pesin and others since the 1970's, but one of the big problems of the theory is the difficulty in verifying the conditions of non-uniform hyperbolicity in specific examples, or even constructing examples satisfying such conditions.
In this course we will focus on the first milestone result of Jakobson in this direction, showing that there exist a positive measure Cantor set of parameters for which the one-dimensional quadratic family f_a(x) = x^2 -a is non-uniformly hyperbolic. The course will be based on an expository account of the proof given in my paper "Bounded recurrence of critical points and Jakobson's Theorem", In: The Mandelbrot set: themes and variations, London Mathematical Society Lecture Notes (Tan Lei ed.), Cambridge University Press, 2000. pp. http://www.ma.ic.ac.uk/~luzzatto/StefIC/papers/l2.PDF
