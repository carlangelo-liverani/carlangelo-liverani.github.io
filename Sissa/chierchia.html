KAM and Nekhoroshev techniques in Celestial Mechanics

abstract: recent developments in KAM and Nekhoroshev theory, motivated by "degeneracies" arising in Celestial Mechanics, are discussed. In particular, KAM (computer assisted) stability proofs for a restricted three body problem (extracted from the Solar System) and Nekhoroshev estimates for the D'Alembert model of spin:orbit resonances are presented.
