BIRKHOFF NORMAL FORM FOR SOME NONLINEAR PDE's

Abstarct: One of the most usefull tools in studying the dynamics of Hamiltonian systems close to elliptic equilibria is Birkhoff normal form theorem which ensures that in suitable coordinates (and up to a small remainder) the hamiltonian of the system depends only on the actions of the linearized system. In particular it follows that the energies of the single normal modes are approximatively constants of motion. The problem of extending to PDEs such a result is considered. As a model problem I will take the nonlinear wave equation $$ u_{tt}-u_{xx}+g(x,u)=0 $$ with Dirichlet boundary conditions on $[0,\pi]$; $g$ is an analytic skewsymmetric function which vanishes for $u=0$ and is periodic with period $2\pi$ in the $x$ variable. After recalling and discussing the classical results on Birkhoff normal form I will present a a result ensuring that, under a nonresonance condition which is fulfilled for most $g$, there exists a canonical transformation that puts the Hamiltonian in Birkhoff normal form up to a reminder of arbitrary high order. The canonical transformation is well defined in a neighbourhood of the origin of a Sobolev type phase space of sufficiently high order. Some dynamical consequences are obtained. The technique of proof is applicable to quite general equations in one space dimension.
