Hyperbolicity versus partial hyperboliciy

Abstract: The set of dynamical systems is well understood if we restrict to hyperbolic or elliptic behaviors. In general, these two kinds of behavior coexist, and we have no tools to study them. A possible way is to look for invariant objects which are partially hyperbolic. In this case, we can ask for geometric and analytic conditions under which, the dynamics in the neighborhood of the object is hyperbolic. We discuss an example : if T is a partially hyperbolic torus of a Hamiltonian systems, with a transversal homoclinic connexion, and the flow on T is with torsion, then the dynamics near T is hyperbolic. This phenomenon (creation of hyperbolicity) is certainly not isolated. A possible programm is to look for similar phenomenons for general partially hyperbolic objetcs.
