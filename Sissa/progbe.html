Bessi: An introduction to Aubry-Mather theory.

The course is intended as a short introduction to the Aubry-Mather theory; we will start from the low dimensional situation of twist maps and show how variational methods apply to them in a natural way. In higher dimensional situations we will see the interplay between variational and ergodic ideas in the Aubry-Mather theory. We shall also see how some analytical ideas like viscosity solutions are useful to study the dynamics of Hamiltonian Systems. Twist maps: what they are, why to study them, how to find their periodic orbits variationally. Higher-dimensional situations: invariant tori, action-minimizing measures and their properties. Mather's barrier and Arnold's diffusion. Mather's barrier and viscosity solutions of the Hamilton-Jacobi equation. The method of vanishing viscosity.
