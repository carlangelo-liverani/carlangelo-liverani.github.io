Hyperbolic Dynamical Systems

C.Liverani


Definition and basic properties of Hyperbolic Systems. How to establish the hyperbolicity of a dynamical system. How to establish ergodicity and other statistical properties in a simple example. Discussion of the properties of the invariant distributions and invariant foliations. Examples of relevant systems (geodesic flow, billiards, expanding systems ....). General results concerning ergodicity and stronger statistical properties.
