A new functional analysis approach to Arnold diffusion

Abstract:
We prove, using a variational approach, the existence of Arnold diffusion with diffusion time $ (1/\mu) \log (1 / \mu )$ for a non-isochronous, apriori-unstable Hamiltonian system with a trigonometric polynomial, which does not preserve the unperturbed tori, as perturbation. A big novelty of our method w.r.t to the classical one's is that we do not use the existence of a "transition chain".
