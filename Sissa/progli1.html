Introduction to Dynamical Systems

C.Liverani


The course aims at introducing some basic ideas of smooth ergodic theory with a particular emphasis on hyperbolic theory. To introduce the subject we will start by analyzing a very simple model: a forced pendulum. This will allow to investigate some basic ideas such as the existence of invariant manifolds (Hadamard-Perron), their homoclinic intersections (Melnikov) and the existence of complex motions (Horseshoe). Motivated by this analysis we will introduce and discuss some standard models (Bernoulli shifts, Arnold cat, Baker ...). We will then introduce some basic properties and definitions of measurable dynamical systems (ergodicity, mixing...) and some fundamental theorems (Birkhoff, Von Neumann, Poincar\`e...). In conclusion we will discuss in detail the statistical properties of the simplest model of hyperbolic system: the toral automorphism (Arnold cat).
