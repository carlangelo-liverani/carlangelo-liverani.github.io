                     Decay of Correlations
                             by
                     Carlangelo Liverani

ABSTRACT: 
This paper offers a new unified approach for studying statistical 
properties of hyperbolic dynamical systems.
The approach is based on a technique that permits an investigation 
of the convergence of a semigroup of operators by
using an adapted Hilbert metric. An exponential rate of decay
of correlations is proven in several classes of examples.

PUBLISHED IN:
Annals of Mathematics 142, pp. 239-301 (1995).
