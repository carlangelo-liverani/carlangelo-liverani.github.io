Smooth Invariant Cone Fields: Errata Corrige to ``Smooth Anosov Flows: Correlation Spectra and Stability"


ABSTRACT: The Lemma 7.2 of the paper ``Smooth Anosov Flows: Correlation 
Spectra and Stability'' was incorrect. Here we fix the error. As part of 
the solution we show that it is possible to construct smooth invariant 
cone fields for  Anosov flows.
