                              Carlangelo Liverani 

ON THE WORK AND VISION OF DMITRY DOLGOPYAT.
          
  
                           ABSTRACT
                                           
I will present some of the results and techniques due to Dolgopyat.The presentation will be non technical and centered on the basic ideas. Also, Iwill try to present Dolgopyat's work in the context of a research program aimedat enlightening the relations between Dynamical Systems and Non-EquilibriumStatistical Mechanics.
