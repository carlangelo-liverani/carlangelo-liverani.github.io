               DECAY OF CORRELATIONS IN HYPERBOLIC SYSTEMS
                                by
                          Carlangelo Liverani

ABSTRACT:
I illustrate a unified approach to the study of 
the decay of correlations in hyperbolic dynamical systems.

PUBLISHED IN:
Proceedings of the International Conference on Dynamical Systems and 
Chaos--Tokyo 94, World Scientific Publ. Co., Singapore, pp. 163-166 (1995).
