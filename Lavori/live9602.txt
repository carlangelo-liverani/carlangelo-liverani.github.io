            CENTRAL LIMIT THEOREM FOR DETERMINISTIC SYSTEMS
                                 by
                          Carlangelo Liverani

ABSTRACT:
The paper contains general condtions under which the Central Limit Theorem holds.
Such condition are applicable to deterministic systems such as expanding maps or
hyperbolic dynamics.

PUBLISHED IN:
International Conference on Dynamical Systems, Montevideo 1995,
a tribute to Ricardo Mane, Pitman Research Notes in Mathemaics
